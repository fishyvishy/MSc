# My Journal Club Presentation

Paper: [Nanoscale NMR of DNA-Functionalized Diamond Using Individual NV Centers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05570)


## Abstract/intro

Problem: NMR and EPR spectroscopy commonly used to probe chemical structure, but require large ensembles to overcome "thermal polarization" and sensitivity issues. Results in averaging over heterogenous conformations 

NV-NMR requires shallow (<10nm deep) NV centers to detect nuclear spins
Ultra shallow reduces coherence, sensitivity


Paper Goal:
- Demonstrate few-molecule NV-NMR of F19 labelled DNA molecules using different methodologies
- Use 2 fluorination chemistries "half" and "fully" fluorinated 
- Different capping methods?
- Tethering chemistries
- Verify resultss using spin simulations

Conclusions
- identify NV sensitivity, surface heterogeneity, and molecular stability as obstacles for NV-NMR in structure interrogation in single-biomolecule regime.
