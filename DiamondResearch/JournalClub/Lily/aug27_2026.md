# Journal Club First Meeting

Paper: [Unconditional full vector magnetometry using spin
selectivity in Nitrogen Vacancy centers in diamond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12090) 

## Intro 
- NV sensors for magnetometry required knowledge of magnetic field (calibrated bias field?)
- Paper shows method for `Unconditional Vector Magnetometry' 
    - no need for external info on field 

- Use elliptically polarized MW fields to select directions of spin
