## Characteristic wastes 
1. Ignitability (D001)
    can readily catch fire. open/closed cup test for flash point, oxidizers

2. Corrosivity (D002) 
    acidic/alkaline wastes that can corrode/dissolve flesh, metal, materials 
    Determined by pH 

3. Reactivity (D003)
    capable of explosion or violent reactions. 
    reacts with water, decomposes under heat or norma handling conditions
    can generate sulfide/cyanide gases under acidic conditions

4. Toxicity (D004)
    wastes that could leach toxic compounds into groundwater from regular landfills

## Chemical Waste 
### Streams 
1. Bulk Solvent Waste 
2. Bulk Corrosive Waste 
3. Chemical labpacks 
4. HHW


### Bulk Liquid Waste 
- White containers: flammable, toxic solvents, aqueous organic waste, ph 7 
- Yellow Containers: corrosive, inorganic, oxidizing, acidic, reducing, caustic pH1, pH114
- or used rinsed, reused old chemical bottles as long as waste compatible with original contents and container material 

- max 3/4 full, leave space for thermal expansion. 
- don't cross-contaminate containers
- only liquids 
- no hot liquids 
- no acutely toxic or reactive waste 

- tight fitting cap with seal 

### Lab Packs 
- materials in the original container, unused, off-spec, expired 


## Misc 
- keep mercury on its own. bad for environment. Found in old electronics 
- keep cyanide, arsenic, strychnine on their own

## Potentially Explosive Chemicals (PECs)
1. Chemicals known to be explosive 
2. chemicals that can form dangerous levels of (shock sensitive) peroxides from prolonged storage or auto-polymerize uncontrollably
3. chemicals that can degrade and release gas -> build up pressure in container

label with date opened and expiration date

# Biomedical Waste 
1. human anatomical waste body parts and organs (excluding hair, teeth, nails, blood and biological liquids)
2. animal anatomical waste ... (excluding hair, teeth, claws, feathers,hooves)
3. cytotoxic waste: composed of drug designed to damage/kill cells
4. non-anatomical waste: sharps expoed to biological liquid/tissue
